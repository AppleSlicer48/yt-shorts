# Topic bank (from vidIQ outlier research, 2026-11-27)

Proven Shorts topics: each one went viral for a SMALL channel (views far above that channel's normal).
The daily task takes the FIRST line whose box is unchecked, verifies the facts, makes the video, then checks the box ([x]) and adds the date.
Skip (and check off with a note) any topic whose facts turn out to be a myth or can't be verified.

## What the outliers have in common
- **The winning format is a "WHY" question about a detail everyone has noticed but never thought about** (a hole, a shape, a gap, a weird part).
  The title IS the question: "Why Pocky Has a Bare Handle" (4.6M views, 64x), "Why Is There Empty Space Before the Zero on Your Ruler?" (1.4M, 455x),
  "Why Plane Windows Aren't Square" (894K, 655x, a 971-sub channel), "Why Do Plastic Chairs Have That Tiny HOLE?" (746K).
- The detail must be instantly recognizable in frame 0 (show the object BIG with the weird detail circled/zoomed).
- Answer comes fast; the "real reason" is a practical or surprising one. 25-60 s.
- Food/brand "how it was invented" also works (Pringles 3.9M, Flamin' Hot 5.2M, milk chocolate 867K), and tiny name-origin facts do well.
- Listicles of 3 also break out ("Products Invented For Different Purposes" 918K, 20x).
- Titles: short, curious, no hashtag spam; a question or a "the real reason" statement.
